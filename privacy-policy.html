HappyRhymeHouse Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

HappyRhymeHouse Publisher is a content management application used to help create, manage, and publish educational video content for HappyRhymeHouse.

Information We Access

When a user connects a supported social media account, HappyRhymeHouse Publisher may receive authorization information necessary to perform actions that the user has approved, such as uploading video content.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through connected platforms is used only to provide the requested publishing and content-management functions. HappyRhymeHouse does not sell personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

HappyRhymeHouse Publisher may interact with third-party platforms such as TikTok. Use of those platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens used by the local publishing system are intended to be stored securely and are not intentionally made publicly available.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may revoke access to connected third-party accounts through the applicable platform. Locally stored authentication credentials and tokens may also be deleted to disconnect the publishing application.

Children's Privacy

HappyRhymeHouse creates educational content intended for children and families. HappyRhymeHouse Publisher is an administrative publishing tool and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.
